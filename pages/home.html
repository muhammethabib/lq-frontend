    Lexicography Redefined

    Beyond the classical approach to lexicography, LexiQamus builds a two-tiered digital infrastructure directly on the original copy.
    It processes words in three categories: Headword Subheadwords Related Words
    Headword

    The headword head, listed alphabetically in the dictionary, with its spelling, pronunciation, and meaning provided.

    حركت ارض
    hareket-i arz
    بلا حركت
    bilâ hareket
    حركت اضافيه
    hareket-i izâfiyye
    Subheadwords

    Compound phrases, idioms, and compound verbs formed with the headword, found within the entry's definition.

    سكون
    sükun
    (antonym)
    عطالت
    atalet
    (antonym)
    حركات
    harekât
    (plural)
    حركه
    hareke
    (variant spelling)
    Related Words

    Words etymologically or semantically connected to the entry, along with variant spellings and pronunciations of the entry itself.
