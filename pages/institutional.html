Institutional Subscribers

Universities and libraries that reach LexiQamus through a national license or an institutional subscription.

An institutional subscription gives every member of a university or library access to LexiQamus. Universities in Turkey and Northern Cyprus are covered by a national license; institutions elsewhere subscribe individually.

Turkey and Northern Cyprus

TÜBİTAK ULAKBİM EKUAL

Since early 2026 LexiQamus has been open to every university in Turkey and Northern Cyprus under the national TÜBİTAK ULAKBİM EKUAL license.

On the campus network no account is needed; off campus, a one-time registration with a .edu.tr address is enough. No VPN or proxy required.
